Fueling Innovation & Technical Excellence

✦ Support

Frequently Asked Questions

All undergraduate and postgraduate college students enrolled in any technical degree are eligible to participate.
Teams must have 2 to 4 members: Team Leader + Member 1 (required), and optional Members 2 and 3.
Registration is free for internal college students. External (out-of-college) students pay a registration fee of INR 300 per team. We cover workspace access, snacks, mentoring reviews, and provide a physical welcome kit (file, papers, pen) upon registration.

Updates will be sent to the team leader's registered email address and broadcasted on our official broadcast group.
